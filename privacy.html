Privacy

plain language · v1.0 · July 2026

Your ideas

Your captured ideas never touch us. The skill writes them to a plain file on your machine (or a tool you connect, like Notion). Nothing is uploaded to SUMMIT, and there is no account holding your data.

What this website handles

  • Email address — only if you request the free skill or buy the full one, and only to deliver the files and any updates you're owed. No spam, no drip sequence.
  • Payments — processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Browser storage — the page remembers one thing locally in your browser: whether you turned the sound on. That's it.
  • Analytics — this page currently ships with no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, and no third-party cookies.

The demo on the landing page

The "throw a bolt" demo runs entirely in your browser. Ideas you type there are not transmitted anywhere unless you choose to submit the free-skill form, in which case your bottled idea rides along so it can arrive pre-loaded — that is stated on the form.

Removal

Want your email gone? One message to hello@summitautomation.ai and it's deleted.
